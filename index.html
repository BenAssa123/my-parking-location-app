Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

My Parking Location ("the App") is a simple utility that helps you save your parking spot and navigate back to it, and can optionally detect when you've likely parked and prompt you to save the spot automatically. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, how it is stored, and confirms that no personal data is collected, transmitted, or shared with anyone.

Location Data

The App uses your device's GPS location to save the coordinates of where you parked your car. This happens when you actively tap "Save Parking Location," or — if you enable the optional "Auto-Detect Parking" feature — when you confirm a parking prompt triggered by the detection described below. The App requests "When In Use" location access for this purpose, and uses it solely to determine your current coordinates and to show your saved spot on the in-app map preview.

Background Location (Auto-Detect Parking)

If you enable the optional "Auto-Detect Parking" toggle, the App uses the device's low-power significant-location-change service to allow it to wake up periodically, even when not actively open, so it can check recent motion-activity data (see below) for signs that you've just parked. This background wakeup is used only to trigger a check — it does not continuously track, log, or store your location history. The App still saves only a single parking spot at a time, and only after you explicitly confirm the prompt; the App never saves a location automatically without your confirmation. Auto-Detect Parking is enabled by default and can be turned off at any time from the toggle on the main screen, which immediately stops all background location and motion monitoring.

Motion & Fitness Data

When "Auto-Detect Parking" is enabled, the App reads on-device motion-activity classifications (such as "driving" and "walking") provided by Apple's Core Motion framework. This data is processed entirely on your device to detect a likely driving-to-walking transition and is never transmitted, stored long-term, or shared. It is used only to decide when to show you a prompt asking whether you'd like to save your parking location.

No Data Collection or Sharing

We do not sell, rent, transmit, or share any data with third parties, because no data generated by the App is ever collected or transmitted off your device. All location and motion data described above stays on your device.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, since no information is collected off-device at all.

Your Control and Data Deletion

You can delete your saved parking location at any time by saving a new location (which overwrites the old one) or by deleting the App from your device, which permanently removes all locally stored data. You can disable Auto-Detect Parking, and the associated motion and background-location processing, at any time using the in-app toggle.
