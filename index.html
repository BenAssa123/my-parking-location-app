Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

My Parking Location ("the App") is a simple utility that helps you save your parking spot and navigate back to it. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App uses, how it is stored, and confirms that no personal data is collected, transmitted, or shared with anyone.

Information We Collect

The App uses your device's GPS location only to save the coordinates of where you parked your car. This happens only when you actively tap the "Save Parking Location" button — the App never tracks or records your location in the background.

How Your Data Is Stored

  • Your saved parking coordinates are stored locally on your device only, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults).
  • Saving a new location automatically replaces the previous one.
  • No location data, device identifiers, or any other information ever leaves your device.
  • The App does not use any analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
  • The App does not have a server, account system, or cloud backend of any kind.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, transmit, or share any data with third parties, because no data is ever collected or transmitted off your device in the first place.

Location Permission

The App requests "When In Use" location access only. This permission is used solely to determine your current coordinates at the moment you tap "Save Parking Location" and to show your saved spot on the in-app map preview. Background location access is never requested or used.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13, since no information is collected at all.

Data Deletion

You can delete your saved parking location at any time by saving a new location (which overwrites the old one) or by deleting the App from your device, which permanently removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, an updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
