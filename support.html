Support

Need help with My Parking Location? Below are answers to common questions. If you don't find what you're looking for, contact us directly at abentzur@gmail.com.

How do I save my parking spot?

Open the app and tap "Save Parking Location." The app will ask for permission to access your location once — after granting it, your current GPS coordinates are saved instantly.

How do I get back to my car?

Tap "Where Did I Park?" The app opens Apple Maps and starts a driving navigation route directly to your saved parking spot.

Why does the app ask for location access?

Location access is required to determine where you are when you save a parking spot, and to show that spot on the in-app map preview. The app only uses "When In Use" access — it never tracks your location in the background.

Is my location data safe?

Yes. Your saved parking location is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I saved a new spot — where did the old one go?

Saving a new parking location automatically replaces the previous one. Only one saved spot is kept at a time.
