How do I save my parking location?

Open the app and tap "Save Parking Location." The app will ask for permission to access your location once — after granting it, your current GPS coordinates are saved instantly.

How do I navigate back to my car?

Tap "Where Did I Park?" The app opens Apple Maps and starts a driving navigation route directly to your saved parking spot.

What is "Auto-Detect Parking"?

Auto-Detect Parking is an optional feature, controlled by a toggle on the main screen, that watches for the moment your phone detects you've stopped driving and started walking. When that happens, the app shows a prompt asking "Did you just park?" — it never saves a location automatically. You choose whether to save it by responding to the prompt. If the app is in the background when this is detected, you'll see the prompt as a notification instead of an in-app alert.

Why does the app ask for Motion & Fitness access?

Motion & Fitness access lets the app read on-device activity classifications (driving vs. walking) to power the optional Auto-Detect Parking feature described above. This is only requested if you have Auto-Detect Parking enabled, and the data never leaves your device.

Does the app use background location?

Only if you enable Auto-Detect Parking. In that case, the app uses low-power background location solely to wake itself up and check for a recent driving-to-walking transition — it does not continuously track or record your location history. Turning off Auto-Detect Parking immediately stops all background location and motion monitoring. Outside of this feature, the app only uses "When In Use" location access, requested when you tap "Save Parking Location."

Is my data private?

Yes. Your saved parking location, and any motion data used for Auto-Detect Parking, is stored and processed only on your device and is never sent anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

What happens if I save a new parking location?

Saving a new parking location automatically replaces the previous one. Only one saved spot is kept at a time.
